Source: GSC drilldown export (45 URLs) and coverage export. Nothing was deployed. Content edits were saved to the live MongoDB `articles` collection through the admin API (`/api/admin/articles/update`) and appear on the public site after the next rebuild. Originals are backed up locally (see "Rollback").

## 1. Verification of the classification

45 URLs: 21 blog articles, 16 tool pages (1 recommendations, 5 filler checkers, 4 manga chapter finders, 5 watch-order guides, 1 tier list), 1 My Anime List page, 4 category pages and 3 tag pages. This matches the earlier breakdown.

Coverage export: indexed pages grew from 49 (24 Jul) to 215 (16 Oct) while "not indexed" grew from 70 to 212, because the site is publishing and being discovered faster than Google indexes it. Impressions rose from ~2k/day to ~24k/day. This is a site-wide authority/crawl-budget pattern, not a single broken page.

## 3. Tool landing pages: thin and duplicated content (follow-up work)

What changed (all computed from data already on each page; nothing is estimated or written by hand):
- New `src/lib/tools/insights.ts` and `ToolInsights.astro` add a facts table and prose to each page: filler pages get first/last filler episode, longest story-only run, how the filler is distributed, arc-level filler, rank against the other lists, and series facts (studio, source, genres, MAL score and popularity); watch orders get release span, main-story share, days at 2 hours a day, scale versus other franchises, plus a new FAQ; movie pages get gaps between films, runtime and score spread and a new MAL score column; manga guides get chapters per episode, coverage and chapters left; arc guides get arc-length spread and filler-arc share.
- The generic "Should I skip filler?" FAQ answer, identical on every filler page, was replaced with answers that use each list's numbers.
- Each movies page used to link all 20 sibling guides; it now links the 8 nearest by popularity (every movies page is still linked from its franchise's watch-order page).
- 9 unit tests added (86 total pass).

Not changed: the tools themselves, URLs, titles, canonicals and the indexability rules (your decision on 2026-10-20 to index every generated tool page stands). Remaining shared text is page chrome (nav, related-tools block, share box, credit line) and fixed table headings.

Limits to be honest about: the 251 filler pages for series with no filler are still noindex by the existing rule; their new text is short because there is little to say. Search engines may still treat near-identical structure as templated, so expect a gradual change rather than an immediate one.

Other tool/category pages from the export (tier list, recommendation hub, My Anime List, categories) were left as they were except for the category and tag title fixes in section 2.

## 8. Tool and mobile-performance pass (2026-10-25)

Mobile PageSpeed was 60 against 90+ on desktop. Lighthouse run locally against the live site (mobile, simulated slow 4G) reproduced it and located the causes:

| Page | Live before | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | 55 (LCP 14.5 s) | The hero and three card images were original uploads (PNG/JPEG up to 1.3 MB each, 4.1 MB page) because images without `-WxH` in their R2 key skipped the image optimiser |
| Filler list (Naruto) | 52 (FCP 6.6 s, CLS 0.18) | The AdSense library and its auction requests loaded in the head and competed with the page; with ad requests blocked the same page scores 93 |
| Article (Kaiju No. 8) | 73 (LCP 5.9 s) | Same unoptimised-image and ad contention |

Fixes: every R2 image now goes through the build-time WebP/srcset optimiser (sizes read from the file header when the key has none), including article body images and the author avatar; AdSense loads after the page has loaded and the browser is idle; ad slots reserve their height. On the local production-style build the same pages score 97 to 100 with LCP 1.5 to 2.3 s and CLS 0 to 0.002. Those numbers exclude ads, which cannot run on localhost, so re-measure on the live site after deploying.

Other changes: watch-order titles no longer call entries "seasons"; wiki artefacts in arc names are cleaned (stray commas, "(Arc)", the literal "None"); filler pages gain a skip-ranges callout and a label glossary that states which labels the source cannot supply; watch orders gain a table placing movies, OVAs and specials; "Anime like X" pages gain a comparison table and picks grouped by what the reader liked; articles link to arc and "anime like" guides; tier lists gain eight data-built templates; the daily game no longer shifts layout; contrast fixed site-wide (tertiary text and small red text); tier labels pick readable text colours. 91 tests pass, `astro check` shows 0 errors, and the project's production safety checks pass.
